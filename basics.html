Landing = the foot touches the ground. Toe-off = it leaves. Contact time = landing to toe-off. Swing time = toe-off to the next landing. Stride time = landing to the next landing of the same foot. Variability = spread of the stride time, as a share of its mean. Numbers come from the pod's own step detection, which still misses steps on a treadmill and has not been checked for toe-off. Raw values arrive 50 times a second.
