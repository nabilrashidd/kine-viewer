How the positions are made

Nothing here integrates acceleration into position. A foot only moves when its pod reports a finished stride (forward and sideways distance, measured between two resting moments of that foot). Between landings the foot in the air is drawn moving towards where its last stride says it will land (dashed).

Two pods each have their own heading, and both drift slowly. The model assumes what a walking person does: both feet travel the same way on average, the left foot stays left of the right one, about hip-width apart, and one foot is never more than about a stride ahead of the other. When a landing breaks one of these, both feet are nudged half each, and the nudge is shown above so it can't hide anything.

Step width is not shown: the sideways part of each stride has a few centimetres of error, and summed over many strides it wanders, so the drawing holds the feet at a normal hip width instead. Stride length, contact and flight time, cadence and symmetry are each pod's own measurements.

For this to line up, Zero both pods while standing still with the feet parallel. Strides from FREE (in-hand) mode or strides a pod could not track are not used.
